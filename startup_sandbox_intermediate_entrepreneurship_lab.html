Concept #1 📦

COGS (Unit Cost)

Cost of Goods Sold: The direct cash spent to make exactly ONE single item (materials + packaging + direct labor).

Formula: Raw Ingredients + Box = COGS
Concept #2 💰

Gross Margin %

The % of revenue you keep after paying raw production costs. High margin = safety armor during bad times!

Formula: (Price - COGS) ÷ Price × 100
Concept #3 ⏳

The Bottleneck

The slowest step in your business. Having 1,000 customers doesn't help if your 3D printer can only make 8 units!

Constraint: Max Output Speed Limit
Concept #4 🧊

Inventory Risk & Spoilage

Unsold physical food or drinks go bad. Overproducing turns your precious startup cash into garbage bin waste!

Rule: Unsold Inventory = Lost Cash
